04 / Before we open the console

GOOD QUESTIONS.
CLEAR ANSWERS.

Is the service compatible with my model?

Compatibility depends on the model and, for some installations, the board revision. For example, PicoStation is intended for PS1 SCPH-1002, 5002 and 5502, subject to inspection. Send your console details before shipping.

What do the prices include?

Services are described on each card and prices are in euros. IPS services include the kit and installation; the kit and finish are agreed in the quote. Controllers, microSD cards, new shells if needed and shipping are extra depending on the service. Additional thermal materials are quoted in advance.

Does maintenance include fixing a fault?

No. Preventive maintenance is for consoles in working order. It includes internal cleaning, thermal renewal and tests before and after. PS3 maintenance does not include a delid. If your console has a fault, describe it before booking the service.

How long will the work take?

Ask Diego for an estimated turnaround before shipping your equipment. It depends on the service, required materials and availability. International transit times are arranged separately.

How do I arrange international shipping?

Contact us first to confirm the address, quote and shipping terms for both journeys. Shipping is paid by the customer. Depending on your country, customs procedures or charges may need to be checked before sending.

Which languages can I use to get in touch?

Diego provides service in Spanish, Portuguese and English. Choose the website language and your service enquiry will be prepared in that language.
